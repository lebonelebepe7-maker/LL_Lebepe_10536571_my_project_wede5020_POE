## Organisation Overview 

# Name 

Inspire Then Die (Pty) Ltd 

# Brief History 

Founded in 2021 in Rosebank, Johannesburg, InspireThenDie is a South Africpremier luxury streetwear brand. Built around a philosophy of leaving an long lasting legacy, the brand merges high-fashion tailoring with authentic South African urban culture, producing limited-edition drop collections (luxury graphic hoodies, leather jackets,distressed denim, and bespoke footwear). By leveraging extreme scarcity and exclusivity—Inspire Then Die commands premium prices (R3,500 – R18,000 per garment) and exceptional profit margins. 

Mission and Vision 

Misson: To ignite creative legacy and self-expression through ultra-premium, ethically crafted luxury streetwear while empowering local youth in south Africa to express themselves using the clothes that they wear 

Vision Statement: To stand as Africa&#39;s most influential avant-garde luxury label, establishing an unmistakable global footprint across London, Paris, Tokyo, and Dubai via a state-of-the-art e-commerce portal. 

# Target Audience 

Primary Segment (Braamfontein Youth & Trendsetters, Ages 18–24): University students and young creatives in urban hubs like Braamfontein seeking exclusive, culture-defining streetwear that offers high social currency and flexible payment options (PayFlex).  

Secondary Segment (Young Urban Professionals, Ages 25–30): High-earning young professionals across Johannesburg (Sandton, Rosebank, Braam) looking for tailored, high-ticket avant-garde garments with seamless digital checkout and fast local delivery.  

Tertiary Segment (Global Cultural Collectors, Ages 20–30): International diaspora and streetwear collectors looking for authenticated, limited-run African luxury drops.  

## Website Goals and Objectives 

Specific Goals 

The primary objective is to transition Inspire Then Die from exclusive offline pop-up events into a digital e-commerce powerhouse capable of executing high-volume limited drop; releases, processing local South African payments, and automating nationwide courier dispatch. 

Revenue Generation Target: Generate R575K in direct online sales within the first 12 months post-launch. 

E-Commerce Capability: Support automated high-concurrency drop launches with capacity to handle 15,000 active site users simultaneously during drops without crashes. 

Local Payment Optimization: Establish seamless local SA payment options including PayFast, Ozow Instant EFT, SnapScan, and PayFlex (Buy Now Pay Later). 

Website Traffic: Targeting 120,000 monthly unique visitors across South Africa and international markets. 

2.2 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) 

KPI 1 (Monthly Online Orders): Generate a minimum of 250 completed online transactions per month (scaling to 1,200+ orders during drop launch months) across the South African market.  

KPI 2 (Average Order Value - AOV): Maintain an average cart total of R4,800 per transaction by encouraging multi-item orders (e.g., pairing hoodies with limited accessories or footwear).  

KPI 3 (Website Traffic & Youth Engagement): Attract a minimum of 35,000 unique monthly website visitors, with at least 60% of total traffic coming from mobile devices used by youth and young professionals aged 18–30 in key urban hubs (Braamfontein, Rosebank, Sandton, and Cape Town).  

## Current Website Analysis 

Inspire Then Die currently operates a temporary splash page designed primarily for social media links. 

# Strengths 

Design & Visual Aesthetics: Visually compelling, high-concept lookbook imagery that successfully communicates the brand's luxury and avant-garde aesthetic.  

Social Proof & Communication: Direct, active links to social channels (Instagram, WhatsApp) enabling basic one-on-one customer inquiry handling.  

Brand Visibility: Physical flagship store address (Rosebank) and customer service email clearly listed for trust building.  

# Weaknesses 

Lack of E-Commerce Capability: Zero native shopping cart or automated checkout; customers must manually place orders via Instagram DM or WhatsApp, creating significant purchase friction.  

Mobile Layout & Responsiveness: Non-responsive design causes image stretching, text overlapping, and poor navigation on mobile screens (where over 70% of youth traffic originates).  

Manual Payment Processing: Limited to manual bank transfers (EFT), which delays order verification and dispatch by 2–3 business days.  

 

# Opportunities for Improvement 

Implement a mobile-first, luxury editorial UI with clean typography, high-res image zoom, and fast asset loading.  

Integrate full e-commerce functionality with dynamic inventory tracking, size selector, and automated cart checkout 

Automate Courier Guy API for live shipping rates and implement local on-page SEO targeting SA luxury terms.  

## Proposed Website Features and Functionality 

Homepage: Features dynamic video header showcasing the latest collection, a live countdown timer for upcoming drops, curated product highlights, and client testimonials. 

About Us Page: Tells the Inspire Then Die story, highlighting local craftsmanship in Gauteng, brand philosophy, and ethical sourcing. 

Products / Shop Page: Organized luxury catalog with filter options (Category: Hoodies, Outerwear, Accessories, Footwear; Size; Price) and 360-degree interactive product inspection. 

Contact Page: Interactive Google Maps embed for Rosebank Store, WhatsApp direct chat widget, and dynamic inquiry form. 

VIP Early Access Portal: Exclusive VIP lounge requiring SMS/Email OTP login, giving early drop access to high-value return buyers 1 hour before public releases. 

 Automated SA Shipping API: Integrated Courier Guy / RAM Hand-to-Hand API providing real-time shipping costs and door-to-door tracking for customers across SA. 

## Design and User Experience 

# Colour Scheme 

Colour Scheme: Onyx Black (#121212) primary background for mystery and depth; Sandstone Gold(#C5A059) accents for premium pricing calls-to-action; Pure White (#FFFFFF) for crisp readability. 

# Typography 

Primary Heading Font: Helvetica Neue / Arial (Clean, bold sans-serif); The bold weight creates a sharp contrast against body text, immediately drawing the reader’s eye to key section headers and drop collection announcements. Body Text: Calibri/ Sans-serif (Highly readable on mobile screens at 11pt). It maintains clean character spacing and line heights, allowing product descriptions, sizing charts, and shipping policies to look neat.  

# Layout and Design Approach 

Minimalist, dark-mode luxury layout with high visual hierarchy, micro- 

interactions on hover, card-based product displays, and sticky navigation bar. 

# User Experience Considerations 

Mobile-first fluid responsiveness ensuring sub-1.5 second loadingspeed on South African 4G/5G mobile networks.
